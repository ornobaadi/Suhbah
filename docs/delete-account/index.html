Delete your Suhbah account

Suhbah · developer ornobaadi

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Suhbah and go to the Me tab.
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. If you use Google sign-in, you'll be asked to sign in once more to prove it's you.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email ornobaadi.dev@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Suhbah account", sent from the Google account you signed in with (guests: include your nickname). We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in (Google link or guest ID)
  • Your profile: nickname, colour, location and prayer-time settings
  • Every daily log: prayers, ayat, istighfar and good deeds
  • Your place in a pair. Your partner keeps their own logs but no longer sees yours.

What is kept

Nothing is kept on purpose. Encrypted Firebase backups may hold your data for up to 30 days before they are erased automatically.
