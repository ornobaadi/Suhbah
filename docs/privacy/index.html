Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

Suhbah is a shared worship tracker. You log your prayers, Quran and istighfar, and, if you choose, see them together with one partner. This policy explains what the app stores, why, and how to remove it.

What we store

DataWhy
Google account name, email and a user ID (only if you sign in with Google). Guests get an anonymous ID instead.To sign you in and keep your logs on your account across devices.
Nickname and the colour you pickShown to you and your partner on the arc.
Approximate location (latitude and longitude rounded to about 1 km) or the city you choose, plus your prayer-time methodTo work out prayer times on your phone. Your partner can see it so the app can show their prayer times.
Daily logs: prayers marked, ayat and istighfar counts, optional good deedsThe core of the app: your progress, streaks and history.
Pair code and partner linkTo connect you with the one person you invited or joined.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics SDKs.
  • We never sell or share your data with anyone. Only you and the partner you pair with can see your logs.
  • The camera is used only while you scan an invite QR code. No images are saved or sent.
  • Prayer times are calculated on your phone. Your location is not sent to any prayer-time service.

Where it lives

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), encrypted in transit and at rest. Firestore security rules allow only you, and your paired partner for shared fields, to read your data. See Google's Firebase privacy information.

Your choices

  • Change your nickname, colour or location any time in Me.
  • Leave a pair any time; your partner stops seeing your logs.
  • Delete your account and all its data from the app or the web. See how to delete your account.

Retention

Your data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, daily logs and pair membership are deleted right away. Firebase may keep encrypted backups for up to 30 days before they are erased.

Children

Suhbah is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
