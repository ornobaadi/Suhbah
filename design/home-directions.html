Direction 2

Saff

A saff is the row you stand in for prayer. Each member gets one row of five tiles, so the day reads like a grid. Plain white, one serif number.

  • Best for: building in a week; it's mostly a grid
  • Tradeoff: rows side by side invite comparison, against "together over versus"

Direction 3

Day Path

The sun moving across the sky, with prayers as stops along its path. The background follows the real time of day: dawn pink, afternoon amber, night indigo.

  • Best for: a memorable look; nobody else in the category does this
  • Tradeoff: five sky themes to tune, and contrast is harder at midday

Direction 4

Mihrab

The bond's progress fills a prayer-niche arch with gold and a faint geometric pattern. Individual names are hidden; only the shared fill shows. The bond's intention sits above it.

  • Best for: the "no comparison, sincerity first" principle
  • Tradeoff: least information at a glance; who-did-what lives one tap away

Direction 5

Next Prayer First

Built around one job: log the prayer you're on with one big button. Partners and the together score sit underneath. Sage green, Material 3 shapes.

  • Best for: the one-week build and daily speed (P5, one tap)
  • Tradeoff: looks the most like a regular utility app
