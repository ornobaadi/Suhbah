What changed

  • Every step shows its result at once: the avatar, your arc half, the sun path, the two seats.
  • Progress is five small arches that fill, matching the waqt shapes on Today.
  • Location comes before pairing, so the last screen ends on a person, not a setting.
  • Pairing celebrates live: the empty seat fills with their color, the two seats close up, confetti in both colors.

Kept from the app

Same type, same tokens, the arcade background, the new palette and rounded-square swatches. No hearts, no stars on controls.

Motion budget

Every animation runs under 700ms, except the confetti, which plays once. All of it turns off when the phone's reduce-motion setting is on.
